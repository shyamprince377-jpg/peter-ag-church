OUR CHURCH

About Us

Peter AG Church is a Christ-centered community where people from every walk of life can come together to worship God, grow in His Word, build meaningful fellowship, and serve others with love.

We believe church is more than a place to gather—it is a family where faith is strengthened, lives are encouraged, and the hope of Jesus is shared. Through worship services, prayer, cell fellowships, Sunday School and ministry, we seek to help every person take the next step in their walk with Christ.
