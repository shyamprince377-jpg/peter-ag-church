ABOUT US

PETER AG CHURCH

PETER AG CHURCH is a Christ-centered spiritual family where people can worship God, grow in His Word, experience fellowship, and serve others with the love of Jesus Christ.

Through worship, prayer, fellowship, Sunday School and ministry, we seek to encourage every person to grow in faith and discover God’s purpose for their life. You are warmly welcome to worship and grow with us.
