ABOUT US

PETER AG CHURCH

PETER AG CHURCH is a Christ-centered community committed to worshipping God, studying His Word, and sharing His love with our neighbours and the world.

We believe in the power of prayer, fellowship and the transforming grace of Jesus Christ. Everyone is welcome to come as they are, grow in faith and serve with love.
